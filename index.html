TRUST & SAFETY

Privacy center

Understand what this prototype stores and how to use it safely.

⬡

Local demo storage

Incident records are stored in this browser using localStorage. They are not sent to a server by this prototype.

◉

Data minimization

Use fictional identifiers and masked contact details. Avoid real identity documents and financial account information.

⌘

Human review

Matching values can be coincidental, recycled or inaccurate. Never use a match to accuse, identify or target a person.

▤

Evidence index

This demo stores evidence names and notes only, not uploaded file contents.

Reset demo workspace

Delete locally saved reports and restore the original fictional dataset.

Important limitation

CyberLink is a hackathon prototype, not an official cybercrime reporting portal, forensic tool or law-enforcement database. It does not verify bank transactions, identify people, access government records, track phones or vehicles, or submit complaints to authorities.
